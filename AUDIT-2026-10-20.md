## Remaining limits

These are Chrome emulation tests, not physical Android/iOS verification. Native PDF rendering/printing, actual OS installation, biometrics and other browsers require device checks. The uploaded PDF fixture checks storage and viewer wiring, not a complete real document renderer. Swipe is exercised with pointer events rather than a physical finger.

Customer matching uses normalized names, so different people with identical names need distinct names for now. Customer profit depends on recorded/linked costs; unallocated overhead and missing costs are excluded. Browser storage and the hidden Developer password do not replace server authorization or external backups.
